PROJECT SUMMARY
================================================================================

The SmartHome app has been successfully redesigned from a developer-oriented 
provisioning tool into a modern, consumer-friendly smart home application.

All technical complexity is now hidden behind a polished, intuitive interface
that feels premium and professional - similar to commercial smart home apps
like Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and Apple Home.

The internal architecture remains completely unchanged for scalability and
maintainability.

USER FLOW TRANSFORMATION
================================================================================

BEFORE (Developer-Oriented):
  App Launch
    ↓
  Home Screen (immediate)
    ↓
  Raw Android permission dialogs (no explanation)
    ↓
  Tap "+" → BLE Scan Screen
    ↓
  Manual "Scan" button
    ↓
  Devices with RSSI values and signal badges
    ↓
  Select device → WiFi Setup
    ↓
  Provisioning Status Log (technical messages)
    ↓
  Raw state machine names visible
    ↓
  Firmware status strings visible
    ↓
  Success (no celebration)

AFTER (Consumer-Friendly):
  App Launch
    ↓
  Startup Animation (3.5 seconds)
    ↓
  Permission Explanation Screen
    ↓
  User taps "Continue"
    ↓
  System permission dialogs (with context)
    ↓
  Home Dashboard
    ↓
  Tap "+" → Nearby Devices
    ↓
  Devices appear automatically
    ↓
  Select device → WiFi Setup
    ↓
  Modern Connecting Animation (friendly messages)
    ↓
  Success Screen with Celebration
    ↓
  "Go to Dashboard" → Device appears
